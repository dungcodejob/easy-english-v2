Easy English là Language Learning SaaS hỗ trợ học từ vựng bằng: Flashcard, Spaced Repetition (FSRS), Quiz, Study Session, Workspace multi-tenant, Progress tracking. Hệ thống được thiết kế theo: DDD, CQRS, Multi-tenant, Modular architecture. User Journey includes: Authentication, Workspace Onboarding (4 steps), Dashboard (streaks, cards due), Learning Section (Words, Topics, Study, Create), Study Hub (Quick Start, Source selection, Configuration), Study Modes (Practice, Review/FSRS, Quiz), Flashcard System (Dictionary & Custom), FSRS Algorithm integration, Immersive Study Session, Session Summary, Study Stats, Topics management, and Multi-workspace support.
