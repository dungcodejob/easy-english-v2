Resilience

noun | /rɪˈzɪliəns/

Definitions

Context: Psychology

The capacity to recover quickly from difficulties; toughness.

Context: Physics / Materials

The ability of a substance or object to spring back into shape.

    "She showed great resilience in the face of adversity."
