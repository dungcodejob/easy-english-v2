Serendipity

/ˌserənˈdipədē/ • noun

Mastery Level 78%

Last practiced 2 days ago. You're doing great!

Definition

The occurrence and development of events by chance in a happy or beneficial way. It is the faculty or phenomenon of finding valuable or agreeable things not sought for.

Examples

  • check_circle "Nature has created many miracles by pure serendipity, such as the accidental discovery of penicillin."
  • check_circle "Technical innovation is often the result of serendipity combined with sharp observation."
  • check_circle "We found this charming little cafe by pure serendipity while wandering the backstreets of Florence."

Origins

Coined by Horace Walpole in 1754 from the Persian fairy tale The Three Princes of Serendip, whose heroes were always making discoveries.

Synonyms

Fluke Chance Good Luck Fortuity Coincidence Blessing

Antonyms

Misfortune Bad Luck Design

Phrases & Idioms

A stroke of serendipity

A sudden and unexpected piece of good fortune.

Happy accident

An unplanned event that has a positive outcome.

Pure serendipity

Used to emphasize that something happened entirely by luck.
