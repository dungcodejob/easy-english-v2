Resilience

noun | /rɪˈzɪliəns/ | location_on US / UK

Definitions & Translations

1
Psychology (noun)

The capacity to recover quickly from difficulties; toughness.

Sự kiên cường, khả năng phục hồi nhanh chóng.

2
Physics (noun)

The ability of a substance or object to spring back into shape; elasticity.

Tính đàn hồi, sức bật.

Usage Examples

  • format_quote

    "She showed great resilience in the face of adversity, refusing to give up on her dreams."

    — Personal Memoir, 2023

  • format_quote

    — Financial Times
